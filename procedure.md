# Book IV — Procedure & Enforcement

*How the Code is adjudicated and enforced.*

## Title 1 — Courts

### § 101. The Judiciary
Maintainer review of pull requests is the trial court of the polity.
Judgments are the merge or rejection decision plus its stated rationale.

### § 102. Appellate review
Contested judgments may be escalated to the `amendments` repository as a
proposed clarification of the underlying article.

### § 103. Precedent
Merged amendments and closed enforcement cases form binding precedent,
indexed in the `enforcement` repository.

## Title 2 — Due Process

### § 201. Notice
No penalty without a written charge stating the article violated and the
conduct alleged.

### § 202. Hearing
The accused may respond in the enforcement issue thread within 7 days.
Silence is treated as waiver, not admission (see `law-silence`).

### § 203. Burden
The Executive bears the burden of proving violation by a preponderance of
merged evidence.

### § 204. Counsel
Any contributor may assist another's defense; bots may not serve as counsel.

## Title 3 — Evidence

### § 301. Admissible evidence
Commit history, CI logs, issue/PR records, and timestamps.

### § 302. Hearsay
Screenshots and chat logs are admissible only if the original is unavailable.

### § 303. Chain of custody
Force-pushed or rewritten history is inadmissible for the rewritten period
unless corroborated by external logs.

## Title 4 — Enforcement

### § 401. Enforcement arm
Repository maintainers execute penalties: branch protection, suspension,
removal of collaborator status.

### § 402. Proportionality
Penalties must fit the offense (Penal Code §401). Excessive penalties are
voidable on appeal.

### § 403. Amnesty
First-time minor offenses (vandalism, nuisance) may be resolved by
community-service contributions: documentation, tests, or issue triage.

## Title 5 — External Reference

Where this Book is silent, procedure defaults to ordinary open-source
community norms, then to the public-domain procedures catalogued in the
reference corpus (`supreme-court`, `us-code` Title 28 analogues).
