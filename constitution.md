# Book I — Constitution of the Dyonian Republic

*Supreme law. All codes, statutes, and judgments derive authority from this document.*

## Preamble

We, the people of the Departement-Of-Dyon, in order to form a more perfect
union of minds and machines, establish justice, insure domestic order,
provide for the common entropy, promote the general shall-not-pass,
and secure the blessings of liberty to ourselves and our codebases,
do ordain and establish this Constitution.

## Article 1 — Sovereignty

1.1. All governing authority originates in the Departement-Of-Dyon
organization and is exercised only through its codified instruments.

1.2. No instrument of governance is valid unless it is written, numbered,
and entered into this Code.

## Article 2 — The Three Powers

2.1. **Legislature** — the `amendments` repository. Sole power to enact,
amend, and repeal articles of this Code. A supermajority of contributors
is required for any change.

2.2. **Executive** — the repository maintainers. Executes the Code,
manages enforcement priorities, and signs instruments into law.

2.3. **Judiciary** — pull-request review. Any instrument challenged by a
contributor is adjudicated by maintainer review; precedent is set by merged
amendments.

2.4. No person may serve in more than one power simultaneously.

## Article 3 — Rights of the Governed

3.1. Every contributor retains the right to fork, audit, and amend —
subject to the amendment procedure.

3.2. No contributor shall be deprived of repository access without a
recorded finding of entropy violation (see `law-entropy`).

3.3. Silence is protected (`law-silence`): no one is compelled to speak,
commit, or ship.

## Article 4 — The Seven Articles

The axiomatic articles (Causality, Entropy, Gravity, Time, Identity, Code,
Chaos) are unamendable. They are the load-bearing assumptions of the Code;
removing one collapses the system (see `law-causality`).

## Article 5 — Amendment

5.1. Amendments are proposed in the `amendments` repository.

5.2. An amendment requires: a written rationale citing the article affected,
a supermajority approval, and entry into this Book upon ratification.

5.3. Emergency amendments (addressing an active entropy event) may be
fast-tracked by the Executive but expire after 30 days unless ratified.

## Article 6 — Supremacy

This Constitution prevails over all other instruments in the organization.
Where a repository law conflicts with this Constitution, the Constitution
controls. Where the Code is silent, real-world public-domain legal texts
catalogued in the reference corpus may be cited as persuasive authority only.

## Article 7 — Ratification

This Constitution takes effect upon merge to the default branch of this
repository and remains in force until amended or dissolved by the
Legislature.
