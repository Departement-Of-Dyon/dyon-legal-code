# Book III — Penal Code

*Defines offenses against the polity and their penalties.*

## Title 1 — Offenses Against the Code

### § 101. Treason
Attempting to dissolve the organization, delete the canonical Code, or
transfer control of repositories to outside parties without Legislature
approval. *Penalty: permanent ban, archive of contributions.*

### § 102. Sedition
Public advocacy for replacing the Code with an uncodified system, or forking
the polity's identity into an unrelated project while claiming its name.
*Penalty: suspension of commit rights for 90 days.*

### § 103. Obstruction
Blocking CI, sabotaging merges, or intentionally breaking the review queue.
*Penalty: mandatory revert of all open work, 30-day probation.*

## Title 2 — Property Crimes

### § 201. Theft
Claiming authorship of another's commits, taking over a repository without
stewardship title (§203 Civil Code), or stripping attribution.
*Penalty: forced co-author correction, loss of stewardship.*

### § 202. Conversion
Using another's API, token, or credential for purposes beyond its grant.
*Penalty: credential revocation, 60-day suspension.*

### § 203. Vandalism
Deleting documentation, breaking links en masse, or committing gibberish to
shared branches. *Penalty: revert + mandatory documentation contribution.*

## Title 3 — Public Order

### § 301. Breach of peace
Spam PRs, issue flooding, or bot behavior indistinguishable from attack.
*Penalty: account mute on all polity repos.*

### § 302. Resisting enforcement
Refusing a lawful revert, ignoring a maintainer's lawful request, or
re-opening a decided issue to relitigate.
*Penalty: repository freeze until compliance.*

### § 303. Perjury
Falsifying test results, CI status, or claiming verification that never ran.
*Penalty: revert of implicated commits, mandatory honest-status attestation.*

## Title 4 — Sentencing

### § 401. Gradation
First offense: warning + remediation task. Second: suspension. Third:
expulsion hearing before the Judiciary.

### § 402. Mitigation
Self-reporting before detection, full revert, and documented remediation
reduce penalty by one grade.

### § 403. Appeal
Any penalty may be appealed by opening an issue in `enforcement` tagged
`appeal`. The Judiciary reviews within 7 days.

## Title 5 — Enforcement Charter

See the `enforcement` repository for the standing enforcement charter,
penalty schedule, and case log.
