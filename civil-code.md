# Book II — Civil Code

*Governs relations between persons and things. Applies where no criminal
offense is alleged.*

## Title 1 — Persons

### § 101. Legal personality
Every contributor, human or bot, has standing before the Code. Bots act
through their operators.

### § 102. Capacity
Minors (accounts under 13) may participate only through a parent or guardian
operator. Accounts suspended for entropy violations lack capacity until
restored.

## Title 2 — Property

### § 201. What may be owned
Repositories, branches, issues, releases, and the goodwill of the polity.
Real-world property is governed by the reference corpus, not this Code.

### § 202. Finder's rule
Work found abandoned in an issue tracker belongs to the finder after 30 days
of inactivity, provided attribution is preserved.

### § 203. Adverse possession
Continuous, open maintenance of a repository for one year confers stewardship
title, subject to the founder's veto.

## Title 3 — Contracts

### § 301. Formation
A contract exists when offer, acceptance, and a merged pull request align.
Verbal agreements in issues are binding if both parties react ✅.

### § 302. Consideration
Every contract requires consideration: code, review, documentation, or a
merged commit. gratuitous promises are void.

### § 303. Breach
Unilateral force-push to shared branches, deleting issues without cause, or
breaking a merged API without deprecation notice constitutes breach.

### § 304. Remedies
Breach is cured by: revert PR, apology issue, or temporary read-only status.
Repeat breaches escalate to the Penal Code.

## Title 4 — Torts

### § 401. Negligence
Failing to run tests before merge, breaking CI for others, or exposing
secrets through negligence.

### § 402. Nuisance
Spam issues, drive-by PRs on unrelated repos, or notification-bombing
maintainers.

### § 403. Defamation
Malicious false claims in issues or PRs about a contributor's work.

## Title 5 — Obligations & Remedies

### § 501. Duty of care
Contributors owe the polity reasonable care in reviews, merges, and secrets
handling.

### § 502. Damages
Measured in reverted commits, broken builds, and maintainer hours.

### § 503. Specific performance
Court (maintainer review) may order a revert or fix-forward rather than
damages where feasible.
